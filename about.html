About Sanjeevani Blood Bank

Sanjeevani Blood Bank

Our mission is to save lives by ensuring a safe, adequate, and timely supply of blood to those in need. We bridge the gap between voluntary blood donors and hospitals through technology and compassion.

Who We Are

Sanjeevani Blood Bank is a non-profit, technology-driven organization dedicated to humanitarian service. Established with the vision of "Life for All," we work 24/7 to manage blood collection, testing, and distribution efficiently. We organize regular voluntary blood donation camps in colleges, corporates, and residential areas to maintain a steady supply of blood components like RBC, Plasma, and Platelets. Our team consists of dedicated medical professionals and volunteers working tirelessly to ensure no emergency goes unanswered.

Our Core Values

  • Transparency: We maintain a clear and ethical record of blood testing, storage, and distribution.
  • Safety First: Every single drop of blood undergoes rigorous screening for safety.
  • Community Spirit: We believe in fostering a culture of selfless and regular blood donation.

Why Choose Sanjeevani?

Safe & Certified

We follow strict international medical standards for blood collection, processing, and cryo-preservation.

Quick Response Network

Our modern digital database helps in finding rare blood groups within minutes during emergencies.

100% Voluntary

We encourage purely voluntary, non-remunerated donations to ensure the highest quality of blood.

24/7 Availability

Our helpdesk and inventory management system operate round-the-clock to fulfill critical requests.
